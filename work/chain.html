Detach and replay

Indexer tracks a hash chain. Parent mismatch: walk to ancestor, emit atlas.chain.reorg, re-emit canonical logs. Portfolio deletes above the ancestor. Balances may jump. That is honest.

Why this shape

If every service talks to RPC, you get three fork choices and a bill. ChainKit is the kit: checkpoints, detach, replay. PyScale exists so "Python is slow" has a method, a machine, and percentiles.
